Combat Techniques & Lore

⚔️ Primary Controls

A and D to move, W or Space to jump (twice for a double jump). J throws your combo, K fires your special, L calls up your signature move. Hold S to guard.

⚡ Pink Overdrive Power-Up (Future State)

Build your Overdrive meter by poofing monsters and collecting gems. When full (100%), press [F] or tap ⚡ OVERDRIVE to enter the glowing Pink State for 12 seconds: +85% Speed, 2X Damage, 3X Faster Cooldowns, and 50% Damage Resistance!

🥊 Top Row Guardians

  • Steven: [J] Shield Bash Combo, [K] Bouncing Ricochet Shield (3 bounces), [L] Rose's Bubble Shield & Healing Saliva (+1 Heart — his canon healing power)! Hold [S] / Down to Guard!
  • Garnet: [J] Gauntlet 1-2 Flurry (POW!), [K] Jet Rocket Gauntlet Launch, [L] Giant Gauntlets Seismic Slam (enlarges gauntlets to titanic size and slams both giant fists down in an earthquake)!
  • Amethyst: [J] Dual Spiked Whip Flurry, [K] Sonic Spin Dash (Quartz Buzzsaw) (complete damage immunity while rolling through ranks!), [L] Dual Whip Violet Energy Geysers (4 rising purple flame plumes)!
  • Pearl: [J] Piercing Spear Flurry, [K] Triple Laser Spear Beam spread, [L] Holo-Pearl Clone Duelist (autonomous fencing ally)!

🔨 Bottom Row Guardians

🔮 Gem Typing & Matchups (New)

Every guardian channels a gem type on each move — the same matchup chart as the classic monster RPGs, using the real Steven Universe typings. Super effective hits deal double damage and flash "SUPER EFFECTIVE!", resisted hits deal half. Landing a move that matches the guardian's own typing adds STAB +20%. Riders: WATER soaks (slows), ELECTR overloads (stuns), FIGHT staggers, FAIRY charms (slower attacks), STEEL/DRAGON shatter armour (+damage taken), FIRE scorches (damage over time), FLYING launches, DARK corrupts.

💎 Gem Power-Ups & Healing Hearts

Defeat corrupted monsters to poof them! Monsters have a 35–40% chance to drop healing Hearts (or Cookie Cats) to restore your HP, while raw gemstones can be collected for an instant Gem Power-Up (+25% Overdrive & +500 PTS) and safely stored in the Crystal Temple!

Play fullscreen with the Pink Star app icon!
1
Open on Phone in Chrome or Mi Browser:
Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as your PC and open the local game link or scan the QR code.
2
Tap "Install App" or Browser Menu (⋮):
Tap the [INSTALL NOW] button below, OR tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome/Browser and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3
⭐ Launch from Your Phone's Home Screen:
The Pink Star icon will be placed on your home screen. Tapping it opens the game fullscreen like a native app and works 100% offline!
⚠️ Xiaomi MIUI / HyperOS Note:
If the icon does not appear on your Xiaomi home screen, Xiaomi's security may have blocked shortcuts. Enable it in:
Phone Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Manage Apps ➔ Chrome (or Mi Browser) ➔ Other permissions ➔ Home screen shortcuts ➔ Set to "Always allow" (Green checkmark).
