## 🚀 How to Install via Local Wi-Fi (1-Click)

### Step 1: Start the Mobile Server on Linux Mint
In your game folder, open a terminal and run:
```bash
./start-mobile-server.sh
```
A **scannable QR code** and your local Wi-Fi download link (e.g., `http://192.168.1.xxx:8080/`) will appear in your terminal.

### Step 2: Open on Your Phone
1. Connect your Xiaomi / Android phone to the same Wi-Fi network as your PC.
2. Open your phone's **Camera** app and scan the QR code on your PC screen (or type the link in Chrome / Mi Browser).
3. The **Crystal Gems! Mobile Hub** will appear.

### Step 3: 1-Tap APK Installation
1. Tap the green **📲 INSTALL APK (CRYSTAL-GEMS.APK)** button.
2. The `Crystal-Gems.apk` file will download in a couple seconds.
3. Tap the download notification, or open your phone's **Downloads** folder in File Manager and tap `Crystal-Gems.apk`.
4. Tap **Install**. *(If Xiaomi asks to "Allow installing unknown apps", tap Allow).*
5. 🎉 **Done!** **Crystal Gems!** is installed as a native app on your phone's home screen and app drawer! You can now turn off your PC and play anytime, anywhere.

## 🔌 Alternative: Install via USB Cable
If you prefer not to use Wi-Fi:
1. Connect your phone to your PC via a USB cable.
2. In your phone's USB notification, select **File Transfer / MTP**.
3. Copy [`Crystal-Gems.apk`](file:///home/wanibur/Documents/Wani%20Works/Antigravity%20Tests/Steven%20Universe/Crystal-Gems.apk) from this folder into your phone's `Download` folder.
4. On your phone, open **File Manager** ➔ **Downloads** ➔ tap `Crystal-Gems.apk` ➔ tap **Install**.
