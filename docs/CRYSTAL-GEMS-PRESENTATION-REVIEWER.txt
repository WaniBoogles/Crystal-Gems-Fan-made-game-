         CRYSTAL GEMS! — GAME REVIEWER & PRESENTATION GUIDE
================================================================================

PURPOSE:
A simple, complete reviewer covering what the game is, how it was made, its
inspirations, mechanics, and Q&A answers for your friend's presentation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE 30-SECOND ELEVATOR PITCH (START WITH THIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Crystal Gems! is a fan-made 2D side-scrolling action beat-'em-up and RPG set
in the Steven Universe universe. What makes it unique technically is that it
was built completely from scratch without a commercial game engine like Unity
or Godot. Using over 34,600 lines of pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and JavaScript,
every single character animation, boss, visual effect, and even the music and
sound effects are procedurally drawn and synthesized in real time through code.
The entire game compiles into a single self-contained 4.58 MB file and a
standalone Windows .exe executable that runs 100% offline at 60 FPS."

3. HOW THE GAME IS MADE (4 TECHNICAL PILLARS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Custom 2D Engine in Pure JavaScript (src/game.js - 28,596 lines)
   - Uses a delta-time (dt) requestAnimationFrame loop so timers and physics
     stay consistent at 30, 60, or 120 FPS.
   - Custom physics, gravity, platform collision, hitboxes, dynamic 2-player
     midpoint camera across a 2,600px scrolling arena, and Low/Medium/High
     graphics quality presets.

B. 100% Procedural Vector & Cel-Shaded Graphics (Zero External Sprite Sheets)
   - Every character, fusion, monster, boss, and particle effect is drawn live
     on the HTML5 Canvas using mathematical 2D vector paths (beginPath, arc,
     bezierCurveTo, gradients).
   - Stays razor-sharp at any resolution (720p to 4K) and keeps the entire game
     under 5 MB.

C. Algorithmic Web Audio Synthesizer (GemAudioEngine)
   - Synthesizes 13 background music tracks (episode themes, boss themes, and
     relaxed menu music) and 40+ sound effects live using Web Audio API
     oscillators and gain nodes — no external MP3 files required.
   - Includes smart per-sound cooldown throttling so rapid continuous damage
     never lags the game or clips the audio.

D. Single-File Bundler & Native Windows Executable (Crystal Gems.exe)
   - tools/build.py compiles all HTML, CSS, JS, and base64 lobby artwork into
     one portable file: Crystal-Gems.html (4.58 MB).
   - tools/windows-launcher/CrystalGemsLauncher.cs compiles into Crystal Gems.exe
     (4.7 MB). When opened on Windows, it embeds the game, starts a local
     loopback server (127.0.0.1), and opens a borderless Edge/Chrome app window
     100% offline.

4. INSPIRATIONS & GAME DESIGN DNA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Steven Universe (Rebecca Sugar / Cartoon Network):
   - Follows Seasons 1-5 across 10 Episodes (Beach City Boardwalk -> Galaxy Warp
     -> Kindergarten -> The Cluster -> Bismuth's Forge -> Frozen North -> Human
     Zoo -> Homeworld Trial -> Change Your Mind / White Diamond).
   - Lore-Accurate RPG Stats (HP, Attack, Magic, Defense, Crit Chance, Crit Dmg):
     In Steven Universe lore, "Magic" is alien Gem hard-light science,
     ferrokinesis, hydrokinesis, and hybrid healing. Peridot and Bismuth have
     high HP/Defense; Lapis has high Water Magic; Pearl has high Crit Chance.
   - 2-Player Revival & Pink Connie Lore:
     In 2P Arcade Mode, a defeated partner's gem floats up during an 18-second
     revival timer and reforms in a 3-second beam of light. When human Connie
     is revived, she transforms into Pink Connie (inspired by Steven reviving
     Lars and Lion in the show!).

2. Classic Arcade Beat 'Em Ups (Castle Crashers / Streets of Rage / Scott Pilgrim):
   - Side-scrolling co-op brawling, wave progression, destructible pillars,
     bounce pads, and towering Boss Fights (1.72x+ scale, threat aura, Phase 2
     rage mode, and 4 unpredictable attack patterns including homing orbs and
     meteor shockwaves).

3. Tekken & Arcade Fighters:
   - 3-hit combo strings, Perfect Parry shield (S / Down Arrow), Dodge Dash
     invulnerability frames (I / E), and a Practice Dojo with a combo analyzer.

4. Marvel vs. Capcom (Tag-Team Hyper Fusions & Cut-Ins):
   - 2-Player Co-Op Canon Fusion (V key at 100% Harmony) into 9 canon fusions,
     plus 1.9-second anime portrait cut-ins on Pink Overdrive / Gem Surge.

5. Pokemon (11-Type Elemental Matchup Chart):
   - Every move has one of 11 elements (FAIRY, FIGHT, PSYCHIC, STEEL, ELECTR,
     WATER, FIRE, DARK, DRAGON, FLYING, NORMAL) with 2x Super Effective, 0.5x
     Resisted, +20% STAB bonus, and status effects (stun, burn, slow, armor crack).

6. Kingdom Hearts / Zelda + Spinel Gem Hearts HUD:
   - Top-left Spinel Hearts are backed by each character's HP + Defense buffer.
     High-defense Gems lose heart life slowly (the Spinel heart dims gradually
     before turning black) and refill when healed.

7. Stardew Valley, Plants vs. Zombies & Left 4 Dead 2:
   - Stardew-style character/villain dialogue boxes, PvZ-inspired clean top
     wave tracker, and L4D2-inspired living menu background slideshow.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TOP Q&A ANSWERS FOR THE PRESENTATION PANEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why not use Unity or Godot?
A: Building a custom engine in HTML5 Canvas & JS gave us full control over
   procedural vector rendering and Web Audio synthesis with zero engine bloat,
   allowing the entire game to ship as a single 4.7 MB .exe and 4.58 MB HTML file.

Q: How do the Spinel Hearts and RPG stats work together?
A: Each hero has lore-accurate HP, Attack, Magic, Defense, Crit Chance, and
   Crit Damage. Physical moves scale with Attack, Gem powers scale with Magic,
   and HP + Defense determine how much damage each Spinel Heart can absorb
   before blacking out.

Q: Are the Story Mode puzzles always the same?
A: No — all 8 episode puzzles use RNG generation on every run so the solution
   changes each time, while visual cues and an optional Hint toggle guide the
   player without auto-solving it.
