## 🎤 1. Quick Introduction (What to Say First)

> *"Good day everyone! Today I'm presenting **Crystal Gems!**, a 2D action fighting game based on the cartoon show **Steven Universe**. What makes our project special is that we **did not use any ready-made game engine** like Unity or Godot. Instead, we built the whole game from scratch using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** with over **34,600 lines of code**. Everything you see and hear—the characters, bosses, special effects, and music—is drawn and created directly by the code, and the entire game fits inside one **4.7 MB `.exe` file** that runs offline on any PC."*

* **How Long It Took to Build:** Around **32+ hours** of active coding and testing

## 🛠️ 3. How the Game Is Made (In Simple Words)

1. **No Image Sprites — Everything Is Drawn by Code (HTML5 Canvas):**
   * Normal games load hundreds of picture files for characters. Our game draws every character, weapon, monster, and explosion using **shapes, lines, curves, and colors in code**. That's why the game never gets blurry even on a big screen, and why the file size is so small (**under 5 MB**).
2. **Music & Sound Effects Are Made by Code (Web Audio API):**
   * We didn't use external MP3 files. The game has a built-in **sound maker** in JavaScript that plays musical notes and sound effects live. Each episode has its own background music that plays automatically.
3. **Packed into One Single File & a Windows `.exe`:**
   * A Python script combines the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript into one single file (`Crystal-Gems.html`).
   * A C# program turns that file into **`Crystal Gems.exe`** so on Windows you just double-click it and it opens like a normal PC game without needing the internet.

## 💡 4. What Inspired the Game?

1. **Steven Universe (The Cartoon Show):**
   * **The Story:** Follows Seasons 1 to 5 of the show across 10 episodes, ending with the final battle against White Diamond.
   * **Character Stats (HP, Attack, Magic, Defense, Crit Chance, Crit Damage):**
     Each character has stats that match the show. In *Steven Universe*, "Magic" is actually alien Gem light power and abilities. Tough characters like **Peridot** and **Bismuth** have high HP and Defense, while **Lapis** has high Water Magic.
   * **2-Player Revival & Pink Connie:**
     In 2-Player Arcade mode, if one player dies, their gem floats up with an **18-second timer**. During the last **3 seconds**, a beam of light forms their body back. When **Connie** (who is human) is revived, she turns into **Pink Connie**—inspired by how Steven's healing powers turned Lars and Lion pink in the show!
2. **Classic 2D Brawlers (*Castle Crashers* & *Streets of Rage*):**
   * Inspired walking left-to-right, fighting waves of monsters with a friend, and fighting **huge Bosses** that are much larger, scarier, and harder to predict than normal monsters.
3. **Fighting Games (*Tekken* & *Marvel vs. Capcom*):**
   * Inspired 3-hit attack combos, blocking/parrying with a shield (`S` or `Down Arrow`), dodging, **Practice Mode**, and **2-Player Fusion** (`V` key) where two players combine into a stronger hero with a cool anime pop-up screen.
4. **Pokémon (Element Types):**
   * Every attack has an element (like *Fire, Water, Electric, Steel, Fighting, Fairy*). Hitting an enemy with its weakness does **2× Super Effective damage** and can stun, burn, or slow them down.
5. **Spinel Heart Health Bar (*Zelda / Kingdom Hearts* style):**
   * Player health is shown as **Spinel Heart Gems** at the top-left. If your character has high HP and Defense, the Spinel Heart **slowly loses color and dims** before turning black, and gets its color back when you heal.

## ❓ 5. Easy Answers to Common Questions

**Q: "Why didn't you use Unity or Godot?"**
> **A:** *"We wanted to build our own engine from scratch using web technologies (HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript). Because there is no heavy game engine, the whole game is less than 5 MB and runs smoothly on almost any computer without installing anything."*

**Q: "Why don't the puzzles have the same answer every time?"**
> **A:** *"We added Random Number Generation (RNG) to all 8 story puzzles so the pattern changes every time you play. We also added a Hint button that gives a clue without giving away or auto-answering the puzzle."*

**Q: "Why are the Bosses much harder than the normal monsters?"**
> **A:** *"We designed Bosses to be much bigger, have more health, a dark glowing aura, and 4 different attack patterns (including tracking orbs and jump shockwaves) so boss fights feel exciting and challenging."*
