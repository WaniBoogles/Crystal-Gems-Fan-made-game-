HOW TO LAUNCH
------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Linux Mint / Ubuntu / Debian
   • Double-click "Crystal Gems!.desktop"
   • or run:  ./crystal-gems.sh
   • windowed mode:  ./crystal-gems.sh --windowed
   • check your setup:  ./crystal-gems.sh --check

 Windows
   • Double-click "build/Crystal Gems.exe"

 Any PC / no install
   • Open "Crystal-Gems.html" in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Brave

 100% offline. No account, no internet needed (fonts fall back offline).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Move Left / Right  : A / D

GEM TYPES — WHO BEATS WHOM  (new)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Every move has an element. Watch the coloured tag on the hotbar: it is the
 element that J, K and L will use right now.

   FIGHT   ➔ beats STEEL, DARK, NORMAL      (staggers)
   PSYCHIC ➔ beats FIGHT                     (dazes)   immune: DARK
   FAIRY   ➔ beats FIGHT, DARK, DRAGON       (charms)
   STEEL   ➔ beats FAIRY                     (armour crack)
   ELECTR  ➔ beats WATER, FLYING             (0.9s stun)
   WATER   ➔ beats FIRE                      (slows)
   FIRE    ➔ beats STEEL                     (burn damage)
   DARK    ➔ beats PSYCHIC                   (+10% damage taken)
   DRAGON  ➔ beats DRAGON                    (+25% damage taken) immune: FAIRY
   FLYING  ➔ beats FIGHT                     (launches)
   NORMAL  ➔ nothing special, never resisted except by STEEL

 • SUPER EFFECTIVE = 2x damage (dual-typed enemies can take 4x)
 • RESISTED = 0.5x damage
 • STAB: using your own guardian's typing adds +20%
 • Landing a hit also applies that element's rider shown in brackets.

 Guardian typings:
   Steven  FAIRY/PSYCHIC      Garnet  FIGHT/PSYCHIC
   Amethyst FIGHT/DARK        Pearl   PSYCHIC/FIGHT
   Bismuth STEEL/FIGHT        Peridot STEEL/ELECTR
   Lapis   WATER/FLYING       Rose    FIGHT/FAIRY
   Connie  NORMAL/FIGHT       Jasper  FIGHT/DRAGON

DIFFICULTY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
   🟢 EASY    7 hearts   gentle story mode, full heal each episode
   🟡 MEDIUM  5 hearts   the intended first run
   🟠 HARD    4 hearts   1.4x HP, +1 enemy per wave, only +1 heart between episodes
   🔴 EXTREME 3 hearts   1.9x HP, 4 attackers at once, half-length telegraphs,
                        +2 enemies per wave, only +1 heart between episodes
   💎 DIAMOND 2 hearts   2.5x HP, no snack drops, NO heal between episodes,
                        +3 enemies per wave. White Diamond's chosen only.

 Higher tiers also cut enemy attack cooldowns (down to 62%) and make enemies
 resist knockback (up to 45%), so crowd-control stops carrying you.

 Click "DIFFICULTY" on the title screen to open the tier panel, or click the
 coloured difficulty pill for a quick cycle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HEALING & POWER-UPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
 • Poofed corruptions drop hearts / Cookie Cats (rate depends on difficulty).
 • Rose Quartz's healing tears restore hearts for the whole team.
 • Steven's healing saliva restores +1 heart (his canon healing power).
 • Collecting a bubbled gem grants +25% Overdrive and +500 points.
 • Pink Overdrive (100%) = 12s of +85% speed, 2x damage, faster cooldowns and
   50% damage resistance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
 • Check a boss's typing before the fight: a FAIRY team melts DARK bosses,
   but a STEEL boss will wall your FAIRY moves.
 • Pair a physical damage-dealer with a different type so the tag team always
   has an answer (e.g. Garnet FIGHT + Lapis WATER).
 • EXTREME and DIAMOND punish chip damage: stack super-effective hits and
   interrupt wind-ups with FIGHT staggers.
